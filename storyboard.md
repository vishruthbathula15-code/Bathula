## Act 2: Breaking Point (Scenes 6-10)

### Scene 4: First Conflict
**Duration**: 1.5 minutes  
**Location**: Their apartment, evening

| Visual | Audio | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Arjun working on laptop, multiple screens | Typing sounds, phone notifications | Cool blue lighting, shadows |
| Priya enters, waits, goes unnoticed | Silence, then frustrated sigh | Muted tones, desaturated colors |
| Medium shot: Priya looking hurt | PRIYA: "You're always working. When was the last time you looked at me?" | Close-up of her face, tears forming |
| Arjun looks up, defensive | ARJUN: "I'm doing this for us! For our future!" | His face shows stress and frustration |
| Two-shot: Standing apart | PRIYA: "I don't want a future where I'm alone." | Growing distance between them visually |
| Arjun turns back to work | ARJUN: "You're not being fair." | Camera pulls back, showing emotional distance |

## Act 3: Reconciliation (Scenes 11-15)

### Scene 7: Rain Scene - The Moment
**Duration**: 2 minutes  
**Location**: Outside Priya's apartment building, pouring rain

| Visual | Audio | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Arjun stands alone in heavy rain, no umbrella | Sound of heavy rainfall | Dark sky, dramatic lighting |
| Close-up: Rain on his face, mixing with tears | Heartbeat sound, soft music begins | Slow-motion raindrops |
| Priya at window, sees him | Gasp, her hand to mouth | Interior is warm, exterior is cold |
| She runs downstairs (fast cuts) | Running footsteps, door opens | Determination in her eyes |
| They stand face to face in the rain | Rain pattering around them | Wide shot showing isolation |
| PRIYA: "You're going to catch a cold." | Gentle, concerned voice | Close-up of their faces |
